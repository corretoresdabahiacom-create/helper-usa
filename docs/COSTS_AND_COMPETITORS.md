# Cost estimate and competitor notes
## Infrastructure (verify current Cloudflare pricing before launch)
- Free tier, no card: Workers requests, D1 (about 5M rows read/day, 100k rows written/day, 5 GB), Static Assets, Cron Triggers, Workers AI daily allowance. Likely sufficient for an MVP with tens to low hundreds of contractors.
- Beyond free: Workers Paid plan (about US$5/month) raises limits; R2 attachments and custom domain add small costs; Resend free tier covers low email volume.
## Competitors (from earlier web research; pricing changes, re-verify)
Connecteam, Workyard, ClockShark, busybusy, Raken, Procore, Buildertrend, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ExakTime, Rippling, Worksuite: typically per-user/month pricing and focused on employee time tracking, GPS, payroll or full project management. Helper Connect's niche: a private per-contractor directory of day-rate helpers, per-day scheduling/rates, partial helper payments, and 24h private messaging, with no per-seat fee for helpers (they need no account).
